Polyglot Game Master teaches <b>Spanish to English speakers</b> and <b>English to Spanish speakers</b> through short games. Everything is built in: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet needed.

<b>Learn step by step</b>
<ul>
<li>Learning path with four units (A1 to B2): short lessons, stories and unit tests. Already know a level? Pass its test to jump ahead.</li>
<li>Every lesson starts with a clear explanation of the rule and its key words.</li>
<li>Each level covers what the CEFR expects: grammar, everyday topics and real communication (ordering, directions, opinions, complaints, formal emails and more).</li>
<li>Bilingual stories with comprehension questions. Tap any line to see the translation or hear it.</li>
</ul>

<b>Game modes</b>
<ul>
<li>Practice and Level Select: translate, unscramble, fill the gap and hunt for errors.</li>
<li>Survivor: strict grading, one life.</li>
<li>Number Cruncher: numbers, prices, dates and times.</li>
<li>Lexicon Onslaught: fast vocabulary and Match Madness.</li>
<li>Island Hopper: memorize useful phrases and recall them later.</li>
<li>Conjugation Gym: the most common verbs in all the main tenses.</li>
<li>Tricky Pairs: ser/estar, por/para, este/esto/aquel, make/do, since/for and more.</li>
</ul>

<b>Stay motivated</b>
<ul>
<li>Daily quests, a daily challenge, streaks, XP and trophies.</li>
<li>Fix my mistakes: review exactly what you got wrong.</li>
<li>Phrasebook of the sentences you have learned, and stats.</li>
<li>Listening rounds use your phone's own text-to-speech voices.</li>
</ul>

Progress is stored only on your device. A backup code lets you move it to another phone or to the web version.
