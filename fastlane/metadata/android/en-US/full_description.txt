Polyglot Game Master teaches languages through short games. Everything is built in: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet needed.

<b>Six languages</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Spanish</b> for English speakers and <b>English</b> for Spanish speakers, levels A1 to B2.</li>
<li><b>French</b>, <b>Portuguese</b> (Brazil), <b>Mandarin Chinese</b> and <b>Arabic</b>, taught through English, levels A1 to B2. Mandarin shows pinyin and Arabic shows transliteration, and you can type either in plain letters.</li>
</ul>

<b>Learn step by step</b>
<ul>
<li>Every lesson starts with a short explanation of the rule and its key words, then drills it until it sticks.</li>
<li>Each level covers grammar, everyday topics and real communication, the way the CEFR describes it.</li>
<li>Mistakes come with a word-by-word comparison and a grammar tip. Other right answers count too, and small typos are forgiven.</li>
<li>Bilingual stories with comprehension questions. Tap 🔊 to hear any sentence.</li>
</ul>

<b>Game modes</b>
<ul>
<li>Practice: translate, unscramble, fill the gap, spot the mistake, and more than 20 other mini-games. Move up level by level, or stay on the one you pick.</li>
<li>Dialogues: real-life conversations (a café, a hotel, the doctor, a job interview…). Answer each line to keep the talk going.</li>
<li>Survivor: strict grading, one life. Boss battles every few rounds.</li>
<li>Grammar Gym: pick the grammar points you want and drill only those, with mastery marks.</li>
<li>Card Clash: build sentences from a hand of word cards to beat three foes.</li>
<li>Number Cruncher, Lexicon Onslaught (vocabulary), Island Hopper, Conjugation Station (Measure Words in Mandarin), Tricky Pairs and a Custom Game you set up yourself.</li>
<li>Type your answers or switch to multiple choice. Add a clock or Time Attack.</li>
<li>Two players on one phone, in every mode.</li>
</ul>

<b>Stay motivated</b>
<ul>
<li>Daily quests, a daily challenge, streaks, XP, trophies and practice reminders.</li>
<li>Fix my mistakes: review exactly what you got wrong.</li>
<li>An original 8-bit soundtrack and sound effects, and Leo the lion cheering you on.</li>
</ul>

Progress is stored only on your device. A backup code lets you move it to another phone or to the web version. Display size can be set from 70% to 150%.
