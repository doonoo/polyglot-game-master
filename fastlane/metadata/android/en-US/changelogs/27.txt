Two players, built into every mode.
• Switch the menu to 👥 2 players and play any mode taking turns: Practice, Survivor, Numbers, Islands, Conjugation, bosses and more.
• Each player picks their own language and level.
• Survivor is last one standing; Island Hopper is a race.
• Phones get a pass-the-phone screen; on PC the scoreboards sit beside the game and Enter moves things along.
• Winner screen with rematch.
