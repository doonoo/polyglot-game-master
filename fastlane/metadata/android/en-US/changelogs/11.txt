Match Madness grows with difficulty: 4 rows at A1, one more per level, plus a bonus row on a 5-answer streak (up to 8). Boards of 6 or more allow two slips. Animations everywhere: cards slide in, tiles and options cascade, pairs snap together, hearts break, streaks and level badges pop, result numbers count up, and buttons lift. Turn them off in the sound panel; your device's reduce-motion setting is respected.
