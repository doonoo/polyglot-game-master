When you miss, the feedback now shows the question you were asked, crosses out your wrong words, highlights the right ones and accents, and explains what went wrong (look-alike words, ser vs estar, missing or extra words, word order). Close misses say so; far misses don't pretend to be close. Grammar fixes: 'soy casado' is now accepted, and about 30 practice sentences that marked correct Spanish as a mistake were fixed.
