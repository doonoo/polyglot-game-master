World Languages level A2 and boss battles.
• 8 new A2 units for Mandarin, Arabic, French and Portuguese: shopping, clothes and colors, home, daily routine, weather, travel, health and the past tense.
• A boss guards every 4 units: no pinyin or hints, 2 hearts, accents count, and the final blow is a full sentence. Beat it to unlock the next units.
• Each language has 4 bosses from its own folklore.
