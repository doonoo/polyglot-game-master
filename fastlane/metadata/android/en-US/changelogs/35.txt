Seven new tap mini-games: 🧩 Gap Fill (pick the missing word), 🔀 Order Up (pick the right word order), ⚖️ Meaning Check (does it mean this?), 🔢 Number Pick and 🔣 Number Match (digits and words) in Numbers, and 👤 Who's Doing It? and 🕵️ Tense Detective in Conjugation. They work in every language, and the answer card shows the correct answer.
