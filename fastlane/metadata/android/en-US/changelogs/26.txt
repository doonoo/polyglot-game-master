Four new languages in the main game, plus a two-player duel.
• French, Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese and Arabic are now courses like Spanish and English (A1–A2, taught through English): Practice, Level Select, Survivor and bosses.
• Mandarin and Arabic: tap word tiles with pinyin or transliteration, or type in plain letters.
• New 👥 2 Players mode: pass the phone, each player can learn a different language.
